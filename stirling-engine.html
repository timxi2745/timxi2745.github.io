Stirling Engine Cart

Experimental Thermodynamics & Energy Efficiency Analysis

Overview

This semester project investigated the performance of a model Stirling engine cart. The project focused on comparing expected energy efficiency with realistic experimental performance.

Objective

The objective was to evaluate the relationship between the energy supplied to the engine and the distance traveled by the cart, allowing the experimental efficiency of the system to be analyzed.

Analysis

Experimental measurements were collected from the cart and used to calculate its energy performance. The results were compared with theoretical expectations to identify differences between idealized and real-world performance.

Results

The analysis demonstrated the effects of real-world losses and inefficiencies on the performance of a small-scale heat engine.
