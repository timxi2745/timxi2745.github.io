Folding Knife Project

Personal mechanical design and fabrication project combining CAD modeling, material processing, heat treatment, and hands-on fabrication.

CAD SOLIDWORKS
Material 1084 Carbon Steel
Process Stock Removal
Heat Treatment Quenching & Tempering

Overview

This personal fabrication project involved designing and fabricating a folding mechanical assembly from the initial CAD model through physical finishing.

The project provided hands-on experience connecting mechanical design decisions with material selection, fabrication, fit, and finishing.

CAD Design

The folding components and mechanical interfaces were designed in SOLIDWORKS. The CAD model was used to establish component geometry and evaluate how the parts would fit together as an assembly.

Dimensional considerations were important throughout the design process because the components needed to align properly as a mechanical assembly.

Fabrication & Materials

Components were fabricated from 1084 carbon steel using hands-on stock-removal and finishing processes. Power tools were used during material shaping and beveling, followed by hand-finishing work.

Heat treatment was also performed, including quenching and tempering, as part of the material-processing stage.

Engineering Takeaways

The project gave me practical experience with the full transition from digital mechanical design to physical fabrication. It also reinforced the relationship between material properties, manufacturing processes, and component design.
