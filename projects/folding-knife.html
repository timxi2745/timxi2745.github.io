MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Folding Knife Project

Personal mechanical design and fabrication project involving CAD modeling, component fabrication, heat treatment, and mechanical assembly.

Project Type Design & Fabrication
Tools SOLIDWORKS · Power Tools · Heat Treatment
Status Completed

Overview

Designed and fabricated a folding mechanical assembly from scratch. The project combined computer-aided design with hands-on fabrication, allowing the mechanical interfaces and components to be developed and refined through the manufacturing process.

Approach

Folding components were designed in SOLIDWORKS with attention to dimensional fit, clearances, and mechanical operation. Components were then fabricated from 1084 carbon steel using stock removal and power-tool processes, followed by beveling, finishing, heat treatment, quenching, and tempering.

Results

The completed assembly demonstrated the ability to take a mechanical design from CAD through fabrication and finishing. The project provided practical experience with dimensional accuracy, mechanical interfaces, material processing, and hands-on prototyping.
