CAD Bridge Design & Load Testing

Structural Design, CAD & Experimental Testing

Overview

This project involved designing a structural bridge using CAD and evaluating its ability to withstand an applied load.

Design

The bridge was developed in CAD with consideration given to structural geometry, material usage, and the expected loading conditions.

Testing

After fabrication, the bridge was experimentally tested by applying an increasing load until its structural limit was reached. The test results were used to evaluate the effectiveness of the design.

Results

The experimental testing provided an opportunity to compare the expected structural behavior with the actual performance of the finished design.
