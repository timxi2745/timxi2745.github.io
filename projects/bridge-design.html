CAD Bridge Design & Load Testing

Mechanical design project focused on CAD modeling, structural design, fabrication, and experimental load testing.

Focus Structural Design
Tools CAD / Fabrication
Testing Load Testing
Type Engineering Design

Overview

This project involved designing a bridge structure using CAD and evaluating the resulting design through physical load testing.

The design process required consideration of structural geometry, material usage, member placement, and the expected loading conditions.

CAD Design

The bridge was modeled using CAD software before fabrication. The digital model was used to establish the overall geometry and provide a reference for construction.

The design process focused on creating a structure capable of carrying the required load while maintaining an efficient arrangement of structural members.

Load Testing

After fabrication, the completed bridge was subjected to physical load testing. The test provided an opportunity to compare the expected structural behavior with the behavior of the fabricated structure.

CAD Digital structural design
Fabrication Physical prototype
Testing Experimental loading
Analysis Structural evaluation

Engineering Takeaways

The project provided experience connecting CAD-based engineering design with physical fabrication and testing. It also demonstrated how real-world structures can behave differently from an idealized digital design.
