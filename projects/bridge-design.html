CAD Bridge Design & Load Testing

Structural design project involving CAD modeling, fabrication, and physical load testing to evaluate the performance of a bridge structure.

Project Type Structural Design
Tools CAD · Physical Testing
Status Completed

Overview

Designed a bridge structure using CAD with the goal of creating a lightweight structure capable of supporting a significant load. The project combined computer-aided design with physical construction and testing.

Approach

Developed the bridge geometry in CAD before fabricating the structure. Design decisions were made with consideration for structural layout, material usage, and load distribution.

Results

The completed bridge was subjected to physical load testing to evaluate its structural performance. The testing provided practical insight into structural behavior and the relationship between design and real-world performance.
