Energy-Efficient Solar Tracking System

An in-progress project exploring mechanical positioning and automated solar tracking.

PROJECT IN PROGRESS
Status In Progress
Focus Energy Systems
Area Mechanical Design
Stage Development

Overview

This project is focused on developing a solar tracking system capable of adjusting the position of a solar panel to improve exposure to incoming sunlight.

The project is currently in the early development stage, so the mechanical design and control approach are still being evaluated.

Current Progress

Current work is focused on developing the mechanical positioning system and determining an appropriate approach for automated tracking.

Additional design, prototyping, and testing will be added as the project progresses.
