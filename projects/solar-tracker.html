Energy-Efficient Solar Tracking System

Renewable Energy, Controls & Energy Optimization

Project in development

Problem

A fixed solar panel does not continuously face the direction receiving the most sunlight throughout the day. A tracking system could potentially increase energy collection, but the tracking mechanism itself requires additional energy, hardware, and cost.

Project Goal

Develop a small-scale representation of a rooftop solar tracking system and investigate whether the additional energy collected through tracking justifies the energy and cost required to operate the system.

Research

Research is being conducted into solar tracking methods, energy consumption, system efficiency, and the tradeoffs between fixed and tracking configurations.

Planned Analysis

The system will be evaluated by comparing the energy collected by a fixed configuration with the energy collected while using tracking, while also accounting for the energy required by the tracking mechanism.

Status

This project is currently in the research and design stage. Experimental results and prototype documentation will be added as development progresses.
