MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

3D Printer Assembly & Modification

Hands-on manufacturing project involving the assembly, configuration, and modification of a custom 3D printer.

Project Type Manufacturing
Tools 3D Printing · Mechanical Assembly
Status Completed

Overview

Assembled a custom 3D printer from individual components to develop hands-on experience with mechanical systems, manufacturing equipment, and additive manufacturing.

Approach

Assembled and configured the printer components, paying attention to mechanical alignment, component installation, and system operation. The printer was then modified and adjusted as needed during the setup process.

Results

The completed assembly provided a functional 3D printing platform while providing practical experience with mechanical assembly, troubleshooting, and additive manufacturing.
