3D Printer Assembly & Modification

Mechanical Assembly, Integration & Troubleshooting

Overview

I assembled a 3D printer from individual components and integrated additional aftermarket hardware to modify and improve the system.

Assembly

The printer was assembled according to the provided design and instructions, requiring mechanical assembly, component integration, and system setup.

Modifications

Additional components, including an aftermarket extruder and LCD interface, were integrated into the printer.

Skills

This project provided hands-on experience with mechanical assembly, hardware integration, troubleshooting, and additive manufacturing equipment.
