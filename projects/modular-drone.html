Modular Low-Cost Drone Platform

Design for Repair, Modular Design & Additive Manufacturing

Project in development

Problem

Small consumer drones can be difficult or expensive to repair when individual components are damaged. Replacing an entire assembly when only one component has failed can also create unnecessary material waste.

Project Goal

Explore a modular platform concept where commonly damaged components can be replaced individually rather than requiring replacement of the entire structure.

Design Goals

  • Modular component architecture
  • Low-cost repairability
  • Beginner-friendly maintenance
  • Reduced material waste
  • Parts that can be independently replaced

Research

Current modular and repairable product designs are being researched to identify opportunities for improving serviceability and reducing repair costs.

Status

This project is currently in the concept and research stage. Design documentation and results will be added as the project develops.
