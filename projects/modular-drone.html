Modular Low-Cost Drone Platform

An in-progress project exploring modular mechanical design, rapid prototyping, and interchangeable components.

PROJECT IN PROGRESS
Status In Progress
Focus Budget Friendly UAV
Area Mechanical Design
Stage Development

Overview

This project is focused on developing a modular, low-cost platform for exploring mechanical design and rapid prototyping in a robotics application.

The design is intended to allow components to be changed or modified without requiring a complete redesign of the platform.

Current Progress

Current work is focused on the initial mechanical architecture and determining how the platform can be organized around interchangeable components.

Additional CAD development, prototyping, and testing will be documented as the project progresses.
